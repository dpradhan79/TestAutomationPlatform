# ADR-010 Autonomous Quality Improvement Loop

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-05

## Context

Traditional AI systems generate outputs and immediately require human review.

This approach creates excessive dependence on manual intervention and does not fully leverage an agentic architecture.

The TestAutomationPlatform aims to maximize autonomous decision making and self-correction while maintaining governance and quality controls.

The platform requires the ability to continuously improve outputs before requesting human intervention.

## Decision

Introduce Autonomous Quality Improvement Loops at major workflow stages.

ReviewAgent shall evaluate outputs generated by other agents and produce:

- Quality Scores
- Findings
- Recommendations
- Improvement Actions

DeepAgent shall use these results to determine whether autonomous refinement is required.

## Autonomous Improvement Workflow

```text
Generate
    ↓

Review
    ↓

Score
    ↓

Target Achieved?

    YES
      ↓
      Continue Workflow

    NO
      ↓

Improve Output
      ↓

Review Again
      ↓

Re-Score
      ↓

Repeat
```
